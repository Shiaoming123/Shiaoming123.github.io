BBC 6 Minute English — "What's in a footballer's brain?"（足球运动员的大脑里有什么？）

每日英语练习 - 2026-07-21（星期二）

📌 今日主题

BBC 6 Minute English — “What’s in a footballer’s brain?”（足球运动员的大脑里有什么？）

本期节目探讨了足球运动员如何在高强度比赛中快速做出决策，以及顶级俱乐部如何借助神经科学（neuroscience）来训练球员的大脑，提高在高压场景下的表现力。

📝 英文原文

Note: This is not a word-for-word transcript.

Georgie: Hello, this is 6 Minute English from BBC Learning English. I’m Georgie.

Neil: And I’m Neil.

Georgie: Neil, I’m not a huge fan of football, but I know you are. So, why do you love it so much?

Neil: Oh, where can I start? I think it’s the combination of the passion and also the skill and athleticism of the players. They call it ‘the beautiful game’ for a reason.

Georgie: Now, football players are faster than ever before, but the game is also about making very quick decisions.

Neil: Yes. To be the best of the best, some football clubs have started looking beyond the body, to the brain. Here’s Tom Cooke, Academy Psychologist for Nottingham Forest Football Club, talking to the BBC World Service.

Tom Cooke: We’re watching these athletes make decisions in split instincts, under the highest pressures and the highest intensities. It made a lot of sense to go, well, neurologically or cognitively, there’s something going on.

Neil: There’s something going on in the brain when the athletes are making quick decisions. If there’s something going on, something unknown and interesting is happening. So, what’s happening inside the brains of football players? And could brain training help win more games?

Georgie: In 2014, a group of Japanese scientists studied the brain of Brazilian football star Neymar. They found that his brain was a lot less active than the brains of his peers when he rotated his right ankle.

Holly Bridge (Professor of Neuroscience, Oxford University): That reduction in activity in Neymar’s cortex reflects that he has absolute expertise in moving his feet. And it’s essentially done with very, very little conscious effort.

Georgie: Decades after this research, football clubs are becoming more interested in brain data technology, even exploring whether a player’s brain can be trained.

Tom Cooke: What we’re doing is trying to design sessions but with cognition or neuroscience at the centre of it. So it could be something like they’re not great at storing lots of information, so we’re trying to overwhelm them, in a safe environment like training, that’s where the development and improvement can happen.

Holly Bridge: A lot of performance is also driven by your emotional state. And that is something you can learn to control.

Niklas Häusler (Founder & CEO, Neuro 11): If you don’t focus right in those moments, and let’s say mentally you’re all over the place, then it becomes more difficult to have an accurate corner and to try to win the game. And when we talk about margins, those are the little margins that can make all the difference.

💡 实用表达

  1. “Where can I start?” — 一种开场白，表示”说来话长”或”我想说的太多了”。很适合在讨论喜欢的话题时用作开场。
  2. “To be the best of the best” — “顶尖中的顶尖”，强调最高水平的竞争。职场中形容顶级团队/人才时很常用。
  3. “It made a lot of sense to…” — “做…很有道理”，表示某种做法是合情合理的、顺理成章的。在表达观点或建议时非常实用。
  4. “That can make all the difference” — “那能起到决定性作用”。强调某个小细节可以产生重大影响，是职场沟通中的万能表达。
  5. “In a safe environment” — “在安全环境中”。用于讨论试错、练习或实验场景，表示在没有风险的前提下进行。

🎯 练习步骤

  1. 通读全文：先完整读一遍英文原文，理解大意
  2. 精读标记：逐句阅读，标记不熟悉的词汇和表达
  3. 跟读练习：大声朗读 3 遍，注意断句、重音和语调
  4. 脱稿复述：合上文本，用自己的话复述大意（中英文都可以）
  5. 结构化输出：用以下框架组织你的复述：
    • 这段话在说什么？—— 顶级足球俱乐部正在用神经科学训练球员的大脑
    • 主要观点是什么？
      1. 球员在高压下做决定时，大脑中有”某些事情在发生”
      2. 内马尔的脚部运动几乎是无意识的，说明 expertise 可以变成本能
      3. 小细节（margin）和情绪状态可以决定比赛胜负
    • 我的感受/看法？—— 原来顶尖运动员不仅练身体，还要练大脑，这个思路在职场上也适用

🗣️ 口才小贴士

本期口才技巧：用”引述 + 解释”来增强说服力

本期节目中，主持人 Neil 使用了非常有效的表达结构：先引述专家的话，再用自己的话解释一遍。例如：

Tom Cooke: “…there’s something going on.” Neil: “If there’s something going on, something unknown and interesting is happening.”

这种 Quote + Paraphrase 技巧的好处是：

  1. 让听众先听到权威声音（建立 credibility）
  2. 再用简单语言帮听众理解（增强 clarity）
  3. 给自己争取几秒钟的思考时间

练习方法：找一段 TED 演讲或采访，听完一句话后暂停，用自己的话说一遍。每天练 3-5 次。
