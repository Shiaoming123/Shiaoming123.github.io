每日英语口语练习：2026-07-30（星期四）

📖 原文：职场薪资谈判实战对话（Salary Negotiation）

来源：改编自 Bilibili 频道「青地瓜侠」《8 Must-Know Phrases for Salary Negotiation in English》实用商务对话，融合 Alex Carter TED 演讲「How to ask for more — and get it」中的协商理念：谈判不是对抗，而是共同寻找最佳方案。

Dialogue 1: The Initial Ask（首次提出涨薪）

Emily (employee): Hi James, do you have a moment? I’d like to discuss my compensation package.

James (manager): Sure, Emily. Come on in. What’s on your mind?

Emily: Well, I’ve been with the company for three years now, and in that time I’ve successfully led the Q4 product launch, improved our team efficiency by 25%, and mentored three junior developers. Based on my performance and market research, I believe a salary adjustment is warranted.

James: I appreciate you taking the initiative to have this conversation. What figure did you have in mind?

Emily: Based on industry benchmarks for my role and experience level — I’ve looked at Glassdoor, LinkedIn Salary, and the recent Robert Half report — I’m hoping for a raise to $95,000, which represents about a 12% increase from my current salary.

James: That’s a significant jump. Let me be transparent — our annual budget cycle doesn’t allow for that level of increase mid-year. However, I can see the value you’ve brought. How flexible are you on the timeline or structure?

Emily: I understand budgetary constraints. Perhaps we could explore a phased approach — say, 7% now and the remaining 5% at the next review cycle? Or alternatively, a combination of a smaller base increase and a performance bonus tied to Q3 targets.

James: That’s a constructive suggestion. Let me take this to HR and our VP of Engineering. I’ll come back to you with a concrete proposal by next Friday.

Emily: Thank you, James. I really appreciate you taking this seriously. I look forward to hearing back.

James: Of course. Thank you for handling this so professionally, Emily.

Dialogue 2: Responding to an Offer（拿到 Offer 后的谈判）

Hiring Manager: We’re excited to offer you the position of Senior Marketing Manager. The package includes a base salary of $78,000, 15 days of PTO, and standard benefits.

Candidate (Sarah): Thank you so much — I’m genuinely excited about this role and the team you’re building. I’d love to make this work. Based on my research and experience, a base salary of $85,000 would be more aligned with the market rate for this level of seniority. Is there flexibility in your range?

Hiring Manager: That’s above our approved budget for this role. The maximum I could go is $80,000.

Sarah: I understand. What if we meet in the middle at $82,500? Alternatively, if the base is fixed at $80,000, could we discuss a signing bonus or an earlier performance review — say, at six months instead of the standard twelve?

Hiring Manager: Let me check with my director. I can offer $81,500 base, plus an additional three days of PTO, and a formal review at nine months rather than twelve. Does that work for you?

Sarah: That sounds like a fair compromise. I appreciate your flexibility. Let me review the updated offer details, and I’ll get back to you within 48 hours.

Key Phrases Summary（核心句式小结）

场景英文表达
开场白”I’d like to discuss my compensation package.”
摆出依据”Based on my performance and market research…”
给出范围”I’m hoping for… which is about X% increase.”
应对拒绝”I understand. Perhaps we could explore…”
折中方案”What if we meet in the middle at…?”
替代方案”Alternatively, could we discuss a signing bonus?”
礼貌收尾”That sounds like a fair compromise. Thank you.”

📝 重点词汇

词汇音标释义例句
compensation/ˌkɒmpənˈseɪʃən/薪酬待遇（含工资、福利等）“I’d like to discuss my compensation package.”
warrant/ˈwɒrənt/使…有必要，应得”I believe a salary adjustment is warranted.”
benchmark/ˈbentʃmɑːk/基准，标杆”Based on industry benchmarks for my role…“
phased/feɪzd/分阶段的”We could explore a phased approach.”

💬 跟读练习建议

  1. 角色扮演：找伙伴分别扮演 Emily 和 James（或 Sarah 和 Hiring Manager），轮换角色
  2. Shadowing 影子跟读：逐句跟读对话，模仿语调、停顿和重音
  3. 替换练习：将对话中的数字（$95,000 → $110,000, 12% → 15%）换成自己的实际情况重读
  4. 录音对比：录下自己的朗读，与原视频对比，改进发音和流畅度

🔄 即兴口语练习

Prompt 1：你的老板刚拒绝了你提出的 20% 涨薪要求，说预算不够。用英文现场回应，提出替代方案。 Prompt 2：你收到了两个 Offer，A 公司给 $90,000 但没发展空间，B 公司给 $80,000 但晋升路径清晰。用英文模拟你如何跟 A 公司谈判。 Prompt 3：用 60 秒讲一个你成功「谈判」的经历（工作、购物、旅行中都可以），用上今天学的至少 3 个词汇。

📋 发音技巧提示

1. 连读：辅助谈判流畅感

  • “based‿on” → /beɪst‿ɒn/
  • “what‿if” → /wɒt‿ɪf/
  • “meet‿in the middle” → /miːt‿ɪn ðə ˈmɪdl/

2. 语调：自信与礼貌的平衡

  • 陈述事实时用降调，保持笃定：“I’ve improved efficiency by 25%↘”
  • 提出请求时用升调 + 稍慢语速，显得礼貌而非卑微：“Is there flexibility↗ in your range?”
  • 折中提议时用平调 + 转折停顿：“Alternatively↘, if the base is fixed…↗ could we discuss↘ a signing bonus?“

3. 重音：关键词加重以传递自信

  • “I believe a salary adjustment is warranted.”
  • “Based on market research…”
  • “That sounds like a fair compromise.”

Happy negotiating! Remember: silence after your ask is your friend — don’t fill it.
