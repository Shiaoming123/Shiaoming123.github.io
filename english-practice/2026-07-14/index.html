📌 今日主题

BBC 6 Minute English — Should We Cycle More?（我们该多骑自行车吗？）

本期节目探讨了骑自行车作为一种环保、健康的出行方式在全球不同国家的普及情况。节目对比了荷兰——自行车文化根深蒂固的国家，与阿联酋——正在大力发展自行车基础设施的国家，讨论了骑行对生活质量、宜居性的影响。

📝 英文原文

Neil: Hello, this is 6 Minute English from BBC Learning English. I’m Neil.

Becca: And I’m Becca.

Neil: Cycling is a popular activity in Britain.

Becca: Yes. This year, sales of bicycles, or bikes as they’re usually called, are up in Britain. Many people choose cycling because it’s good for their health and the environment. And electric bikes are making cycling accessible to more people, including older people.

Neil: In this episode, we’ll visit two countries: one with a long history of cycling and another where it’s just getting started.

Becca: The Netherlands’ cycle network spreads over 95,000 miles, and there are more bikes in the country than its 18 million citizens. So, roughly speaking, how many bikes are there in the Netherlands?

Neil: Let’s hear from Meredith Glaser, CEO of Amsterdam’s Urban Cycling Institute. She says: “The bicycle is deeply ingrained into Dutch culture. But the rise of cycling started in the industrial era, when bicycle manufacturing took off. And it went from a toy for rich people to an everyday tool for transportation, and this was a really important milestone for cycling in the Netherlands.”

Becca: Our second destination is better known for cars than bicycles: the United Arab Emirates or UAE. UAE is hoping cycling could be a greener way for visitors to explore tourist attractions in cities like Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

Martin Tillman: “We also have quality of life and liveability. All the cities in the Gulf are very competitive, and they would love to be ranked very high in those global rankings, and cycling is seen as part of that quality-of-life solution.”

Becca: Gulf cities like Dubai want a reputation as places with a good quality of life. The phrase quality of life refers to someone’s level of enjoyment, comfort and health when living in a particular place.

Neil: Dubai’s town planners are hoping the 600km of cycle tracks already built will make the city more attractive to live in.

Martin Tillman: “It’s certainly not a copy and paste of what is good in Holland or Denmark, but the key is that we’re looking for the principles.”

Neil: Martin uses the phrase a copy and paste to mean an exact copy of something, which is moved and put somewhere else. Dubai can’t simply copy the Netherlands, and a range of measures, including water stations and shaded rest areas, have been created for cycling in the heat.

💡 实用表达

  1. deeply ingrained — 根深蒂固的。用于描述某种习惯、文化、观念在一个人或社会中已经牢固建立。

    Punctuality is deeply ingrained in Japanese culture.（守时在日本文化中根深蒂固。）

  2. take off — 突然流行/成功。非常实用的 phrasal verb，可用于产品、趋势、事业等。

    Her career as a singer really took off after that performance.（那场演出后，她的歌手生涯真正起飞了。）

  3. a copy and paste — 照搬照抄。形象化的表达，不要直译为”复制粘贴”，而是指机械复制。

    Every city is different — you can’t just do a copy and paste of what works elsewhere.（每个城市都不同——你不能照搬其他地方的成功经验。）

  4. look for the principles — 寻找/提炼原则。比”learn from”更高级的表达，强调理解本质而非表面模仿。

    Instead of copying their methods, we should look for the principles behind their success.（与其抄袭他们的方法，我们应该寻找他们成功背后的原则。）

  5. quality of life / liveability — 生活质量和宜居性。两个高频词，常出现在城市规划、职场、社会话题中。

    Good public transport is essential for a city’s liveability and residents’ quality of life.（良好的公共交通对城市的宜居性和居民的生活质量至关重要。）

🎯 练习步骤

  1. 通读全文：先完整读一遍英文原文，理解大意
  2. 精读标记：逐句阅读，用荧光笔标记不熟悉的词汇和表达
  3. 跟读练习：大声朗读 3 遍，特别注意以下语音要点：
    • 「deeply ingrained」中 /ɪnˈɡreɪnd/ 的重音在第二个音节
    • 「took off」连读时听起来像 /tʊkɒf/
    • 「milestone」中 /ˈmaɪlˌstəʊn/ 的 /l/ 要清晰
  4. 脱稿复述：合上文本，用自己的话复述大意（中英文都可以）
  5. 结构化输出：用以下框架组织你的复述：
    • 这段话在说什么？（一句话概括）
    • 主要观点是什么？（列 2-3 个要点）
    • 我的感受/看法？（一句话）

🗣️ 口才小贴士

对比结构让你的观点更有力（Contrast Structure）

今天的 BBC 节目使用了一个非常有效的表达技巧：对比两国案例（荷兰 vs 阿联酋）。在英语口语表达中，使用「对比结构」可以让你的观点更有层次和说服力。

具体方法：用 “on one hand… on the other hand…” 或 “while… …” 来组织对比内容。

例句来自今天内容：

“In the Netherlands, cycling is deeply ingrained in the culture. But in the UAE, it’s just getting started.”

你可以这样用：

“While the Netherlands has a 150-year history of cycling, the UAE is building its cycling culture from scratch. The key difference is that the Dutch see the bike as everyday transportation, whereas in Dubai, it’s more about tourism and quality of life.”

练习建议： 下次你要表达一个观点时，试着先用 A 方的立场说一段，然后用”but / however / whereas”转折到 B 方。这种「对比+转折」的结构会让你的表达立刻显得有条理、有深度。
