每日英语口语练习：2026-08-06（星期四）

周四 商务/职场英语：本期主题「职场中传达坏消息（Delivering Bad News at Work）」。坏消息人人要传、人人怕传——用错语气轻则尴尬，重则毁掉信任。本课取材自 BBC Learning English《Office English》系列 Episode 7 “Bad news” 的用语套路，原创编写了一段管理者与员工的 1 对 1 面谈对话（约 250 词），示范「铺垫 → 直言 → 共情 → 给方案」的专业表达结构。

1. 📖 原文：职场对话（1 对 1 面谈）

场景：产品经理 Emma 需要告诉设计师 Tom——他们负责的 Atlas 项目因预算调整将被逐步收尾。 来源说明：对话为原创编写，用语模式参考 BBC Learning English《Office English》Episode 7 “Bad news”（官方教学系列）及 Episode 9 “Negotiating” 的委婉表达套路。

Emma: Tom, thanks for making time. I want to be straight with you — this isn’t easy to say. You know the board has been reviewing the budget all month. Unfortunately, they’ve decided to wind down the Atlas project at the end of next quarter.

Tom: Wow. I’m not going to lie, that’s a shock. We just hit our biggest milestone.

Emma: I know. And honestly, the decision had nothing to do with the team’s performance. Your work has been outstanding — the numbers were there. It’s a strategic call about where the company is heading.

Tom: So what happens to the team?

Emma: That’s exactly what I wanted to talk through. Nobody is being let go. We’re redeploying people onto the Horizon launch, which needs design help urgently. I’ve already spoken to Priya — she’d love to have you.

Tom: Okay. That’s… a relief, I suppose. When would I move over?

Emma: We’d transition you over the next six weeks, so you can hand over your current work properly. I’ll make sure you’re supported through the change — and if you have concerns, my door is open.

Tom: I appreciate you telling me face to face. Most managers would have sent an email.

Emma: You deserved better than that. You’re one of my strongest people, Tom — this is about the project, not you. Please don’t take it personally.

Tom: I won’t. Thanks, Emma. Honestly, it helps to hear it that way.

3. 📝 重点词汇

4. 💬 跟读练习建议

  1. 先看原片：完整看一遍 Episode 7 “Bad news”（pFP1RW6R08w，12:45），重点听主持人如何用 I’m afraid / unfortunately / we’re going to have to 开头软化坏消息，注意他们说话时比日常对话更慢、更低。
  2. 角色扮演跟读：播放本课对话，先跟 Emma（管理者）全部台词，再跟 Tom（员工）全部台词——两边都要练，因为职场中你两种角色都会当。
  3. 「坏消息三明治」精读：把 Emma 的第一段台词（铺垫 → 坏消息 → 原因）读 10 遍，重音落在 straight、Unfortunately、wind down 上，句尾一律用降调。
  4. 录音对比：录下自己念 Emma 全篇（约 70 秒），与原声对比两处——① 说出坏消息前的停顿够不够长；② “I know.” 这句共情短句是否读得太硬。
  5. 降速跟读 Julian Treasure：用 0.75 倍速跟读 TED 演讲前 3 分钟，模仿他「胸腔共鸣 + 句末降调」的权威感发声。

5. 🔄 即兴口语练习

Prompt 1（90 秒）：你负责的项目要延期一个月，用「铺垫 → 坏消息 → 补救方案」结构向客户口头传达。必须用上 I’m afraid 和 unfortunately，且不能直接说 “We are late”。

Prompt 2（60 秒）：你是对话里的 Tom，老板宣布项目取消后，用三个跟进问题把局面问清楚（团队去向、时间表、支持措施）。用上 transition / redeploy。

Prompt 3（60 秒）：直译改委婉——把下面 5 句改成得体的商务英语：① “You’re wrong.” ② “We need to cut your budget.” ③ “Your report is late.” ④ “I don’t agree.” ⑤ “No.” 用上 I’m afraid / I see it differently / we’re going to have to。

Prompt 4（90 秒）：讲一件你亲历的「收到坏消息」或「传达坏消息」的事（职场、学校、家庭皆可）。对方是怎么说的？哪句话让你舒服/难受？用上 face to face / take it personally。

6. 📋 发音技巧提示

1. 坏消息的「低稳语调」（The Bad-News Tone）

  • 说坏消息时，起调比日常对话低 2–3 度，语速放慢 20%，句尾一律降调。练习：把 “Unfortunately, they’ve decided to wind down the Atlas project.” 用「正常语调」和「低稳语调」各读一遍，感受差别。

2. 关键信息前的停顿（Pause Before the Punch）

  • 坏消息要「铺垫 + 停顿 + 抛出」：“I want to be straight with you — （停 0.5 秒） this isn’t easy to say.” 停顿让听者准备好，反而显得更尊重。

3. 弱读与连读（Weak Forms & Linking）

  • “I’m afraid” → /aɪm‿əˈfreɪd/（afraid 的 a- 弱读）
  • “want to” → /ˈwɒnə/（口语常读成 wanna）；“going to” → /ˈɡənə/（gonna）
  • “tell‿you” → /ˈtel‿juː/；“hit‿our” → /ˈhɪt‿aʊə/；“about‿the” → /əˈbaʊt‿ðə/

4. 句子重音（Sentence Stress）

  • 只重读实义词：“It’s a STRATEGIC call about where the COMPANY is HEADING.” —— strategic / company / heading 三个重音撑起整句，其余虚词一带而过。

5. 咬舌音 /ð/（The TH Sound）

  • the / they / that / this 的 /ð/ 是职场高频音：舌尖轻抵上齿背，气流从舌齿间摩擦而出。练习：“the board — the team — the numbers — the Horizon launch” 连续 10 遍，不要发成 /d/ 或 /z/。

6. 共情短句的「轻降调」（Empathy in Short Phrases）

  • “I know.” / “I understand.” / “That’s a fair question.” 这类共情句用轻柔的降调，音量比主句小 30%——说得太响会显得敷衍，说得太轻才显真诚。

“Nobody likes to hear bad news — but how you deliver it decides whether they hear it, or just hear you.” — 今天敢开口，明天才出彩。
