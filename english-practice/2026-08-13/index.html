每日英语口语练习：2026-08-13（星期四）

商务/职场英语·演讲技巧：Elevator Pitch（电梯演讲）——如何在 30–60 秒内讲清「我是谁、做什么、为什么重要」。本课提供结构拆解（hook → value → ask）、强弱对照范例与一段展会电梯里的实战对话。方法论综合自 HBR《How to Get People to Listen to You》与 TED《The Way We Work》系列观点，适合求职、商务社交与创业路演场景。

1. 📖 原文（核心段落）

来源：综合整理自 HBR Guide《How to Get People to Listen to You》与 TED《The Way We Work: The Secret to Successfully Pitching an Idea》的电梯演讲方法论（示范文本，非逐字转录） 篇幅：核心段落约 220 词 + 场景对话约 120 词

The Elevator Pitch

An elevator pitch is a short summary of who you are, what you do, and why it matters — short enough to deliver during an elevator ride with a client, an investor, or a hiring manager. The strongest pitches follow three steps: hook, value, and ask. The hook is one line that makes people curious, such as a striking fact or a bold question. The value explains the problem you solve, who you solve it for, and why your approach is different. Skip the jargon — if an intelligent friend outside your field would not understand a sentence, rewrite it. The ask is a clear, low-pressure next step: “May I send you a one-page summary?” or “Would you be open to a short call next week?”

Compare two versions. Weak: “I work in digital marketing. We do SEO, social media, content, and some paid ads.” Strong: “I help small restaurants double their takeaway orders without increasing their ad spend. One client grew online orders by 40 percent last quarter. If you’d like to know how, I can send a one-page case study.” The strong version names a specific customer, a measurable result, and a simple next step.

Notice what the strong pitch does not do: it does not list features, it does not use buzzwords, and it does not ask for a big commitment. The goal of an elevator pitch is not to close a deal on the spot — it is to earn one more conversation.

场景对话：In the Elevator at a Conference

A: Excuse me, aren’t you Maya Chen from Northwind Logistics? B: Yes, and you are…? A: David Okafor — I run Portia, a software tool that helps freight companies cut empty mileage by 15 percent. I saw your talk on supply-chain digitisation this morning; the part about route optimisation really resonated with me. B: Glad to hear it. So how does Portia plug into our existing systems? A: It’s a cloud add-on — no new hardware. We can integrate with your current TMS in about two weeks. B: Interesting. Send me a demo link. A: I will — could I also grab your card? B: Of course. Here you go. A: Thanks. I’ll follow up tomorrow. Enjoy the rest of the conference.

4. 💬 跟读练习建议

  1. 热身：先看 Harvard HR 的 1:34 短片（rShZQ93Qi0U），只做泛听，抓「hook / value / ask」三个词出现的位置。
  2. 精读结构段：把「The Elevator Pitch」三自然段各拆成 2–3 个意群慢速朗读，重点处理破折号插入语（“…and why it matters — short enough to deliver…”）的停顿。
  3. 强弱对比朗读：分别用「平淡无奇」和「充满能量」的语气读 Weak / Strong 两个版本，体会重音和语调如何传递自信。
  4. 影子跟读：跟 TED 官方视频（l0hVIH3EnlQ），落后 0.5 秒跟读，模仿主持人讲故事时的节奏与停顿。
  5. 对话角色扮演：和镜子（或录音）分饰 A/B 两角，把对话演一遍，注意问句（“aren’t you…?”）的语调。

5. 🔄 即兴口语练习

  1. 60 秒自我介绍：用 hook → value → ask 结构，现场组织一段关于你自己（或你的工作/专业）的电梯演讲，录音后回听。
  2. 改写练习：把 Weak 版本改写成 Strong 版本，必须包含：具体客户、可量化结果、一个低压力请求。
  3. 对话续写：假设你是 B（Maya Chen），第二天收到 A 的邮件——即兴说出你会怎么回复（接受/婉拒/追问报价）。
  4. 词汇迁移：用本课 8 个词各造一个职场句子，例如 “My proposal didn’t resonate with the client until I removed the jargon.”

6. 📋 发音技巧提示

  1. 重音对比：PITCH 单音节重读；value propoSItion 中 proposition 重音在第三音节 /ˌprɒpəˈzɪʃən/；REsonate 重音在第一音节。
  2. 弱读与连读：问句 “Could I also grab your card?” 中 could /kəd/ 弱读，could-I 连读为 /kʊdaɪ/；“plug into” 连读为 /plʌɡɪntuː/。
  3. 问句语调：反意疑问 “aren’t you Maya Chen?” 用降调（期待确认）；开放式问句 “So how does Portia plug into…?” 用升调（表示好奇）。
  4. 破折号停顿：“…why it matters — short enough to deliver…” 破折号处必须停顿一拍，这是英语插入语的标准读法，也是演讲中的「留白」技巧。
  5. -ed 发音：resonated /ˈrezəneɪtɪd/（/ɪd/）、integrate→integrated /ˈɪntɪɡreɪtɪd/（/ɪd/），注意与 /t/、/d/ 结尾的区分。
