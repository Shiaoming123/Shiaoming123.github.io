每日英语练习 - 2026-07-04（星期六）

📌 今日主题

马丁·路德·金《我有一个梦想》经典演讲选段 — 综合练习。这是20世纪最著名的演讲之一，语言铿锵有力，排比（parallelism）、隐喻（metaphor）和反复（repetition）手法炉火纯青，是学习英语演讲的绝佳范本。

📝 英文原文

Opening & The Dream

I am happy to join with you today in what will go down in history as the greatest demonstration for freedom in the history of our nation.

Five score years ago, a great American, in whose symbolic shadow we stand today, signed the Emancipation Proclamation. This momentous decree came as a great beacon light of hope to millions of Negro slaves who had been seared in the flames of withering injustice. It came as a joyous daybreak to end the long night of their captivity.

But one hundred years later, the Negro still is not free. One hundred years later, the life of the Negro is still sadly crippled by the manacles of segregation and the chains of discrimination.

I have a dream that one day this nation will rise up and live out the true meaning of its creed: “We hold these truths to be self-evident, that all men are created equal.”

I have a dream that one day on the red hills of Georgia, the sons of former slaves and the sons of former slave owners will be able to sit down together at the table of brotherhood.

I have a dream that my four little children will one day live in a nation where they will not be judged by the color of their skin but by the content of their character.

I have a dream today!

💡 实用表达

  1. “go down in history as…” — 载入史册

    • 可用于任何重大成就或事件。例：This project will go down in history as our greatest achievement.

  2. “come as a great beacon light of hope” — 如同一座希望灯塔

    • 比喻某事物给绝望中的人们带来希望。例：Her words came as a beacon of hope to everyone in the room.

  3. “the manacles of… / the chains of…” — ……的枷锁/束缚

    • 极为形象的比喻，用于描述某种负面力量对人或社会的束缚。例：We must break the chains of poverty that hold so many back.

  4. “live out the true meaning of…” — 真正实践……的含义

    • 比单纯说”understand”更有力量。例：We need leaders who live out the true meaning of integrity.

  5. “judged not by the color of their skin but by the content of their character” — 不以肤色而以品格衡量

    • 经典句式，可用于表达公平、公正。去掉具体词汇后，句型 “not by X but by Y” 可灵活套用。例：A good leader is judged not by their popularity but by the content of their decisions.

🎯 练习步骤

  1. 通读全文：先完整读一遍英文原文，理解大意
  2. 精读标记：逐句阅读，标记不熟悉的词汇和表达
  3. 跟读练习：大声朗读 3 遍，注意断句、重音和语调。尤其注意马丁·路德·金特有的节奏感和重复语气的处理
  4. 脱稿复述：合上文本，用自己的话复述大意（中英文都可以）
  5. 结构化输出：用以下框架组织你的复述：
    • 这段话在说什么？（一句话概括）
    • 主要观点是什么？（列 2-3 个要点）
    • 我的感受/看法？（一句话）

🗣️ 口才小贴士：善用”三重排比”（Rule of Three）

马丁·路德·金在这篇演讲中反复使用了”三重排比”手法——这是演讲中最有力的修辞技巧之一。注意原文中的例子：

“One hundred years later… One hundred years later… One hundred years later…” “I have a dream… I have a dream… I have a dream…”

练习方法：选一个你真正关心的话题（比如工作、环保、学习），试着用三个排比句来表达你的观点。例如：

  • “I believe that education changes lives. I believe that education breaks the cycle of poverty. I believe that education is the most powerful weapon we have.”
  • 前两句铺垫，第三句点题升华——这就是 Rule of Three 的力量。

进阶挑战：把这篇演讲中每个 “I have a dream” 段落都读一遍，录制下来，听听自己的节奏感。注意：每次重复时语气要一次比一次更强，而不是一模一样。
