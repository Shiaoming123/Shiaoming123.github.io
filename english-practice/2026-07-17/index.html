📌 今日主题

TED 演讲片段 — Amy Cuddy《Your Body Language May Shape Who You Are》（你的肢体语言塑造你是谁） 核心观点：我们的身体语言不仅影响别人对我们的看法，更影响我们自己的心态、荷尔蒙水平，乃至人生走向。「Fake it till you become it.」

📝 英文原文

So I want to start by offering you a free no-tech life hack, and all it requires of you is this: that you change your posture for two minutes. But before I give it away, I want to ask you to right now do a little audit of your body and what you’re doing with your body.

So how many of you are sort of making yourselves smaller? Maybe you’re hunching, crossing your legs, maybe wrapping your ankles. Sometimes we hold onto our arms like this. Sometimes we spread out.

So when we think about nonverbal behavior, or body language — but we call it nonverbals as social scientists — it’s language, so we think about communication. When we think about communication, we think about interactions. So what is your body language communicating to me? What’s mine communicating to you?

And there’s a lot of reason to believe that this is a valid way to look at this. So social scientists have spent a lot of time looking at the effects of our body language, or other people’s body language, on judgments. And we make sweeping judgments and inferences from body language. And those judgments can predict really meaningful life outcomes like who we hire or promote, who we ask out on a date.

So when we think of nonverbals, we think of how we judge others, how they judge us and what the outcomes are. We tend to forget, though, the other audience that’s influenced by our nonverbals, and that’s ourselves. We are also influenced by our nonverbals, our thoughts and our feelings and our physiology.

💡 实用表达

  1. “sweeping judgments” — 笼统的判断、一概而论

    • 职场场景：“We need to avoid making sweeping judgments about candidates based on first impressions.”
    • 在面试和评估场景中非常实用

  2. “do a little audit of…” — 对……做一个小检查/审视

    • 职场场景：“Let’s do a little audit of our team’s communication style this quarter.”
    • 「audit」不只有审计的意思，也可以表示「审视、检查」

  3. “meaningful life outcomes” — 有重大影响的人生结果

    • 职场场景：“Networking can have meaningful life outcomes for your career trajectory.”
    • 比普通的 “important results” 更高级、更正式

  4. “fake it till you become it” — 假装直到真正成为（升级版「fake it till you make it」）

    • 职场场景：“When starting a new role, sometimes you have to fake it till you become it.”
    • Amy Cuddy 强调的是「内化」而非「假装」

  5. “the other audience” — 另一个观众（指自己）

    • 职场场景：“When you present, remember there’s the other audience — yourself. Your own body language affects your confidence.”
    • 一个很巧妙的表达方式，用于提醒自己

🎯 练习步骤

  1. 通读全文：先完整读一遍英文原文，理解 Amy Cuddy 的核心观点
  2. 精读标记：逐句阅读，标记不熟悉的词汇和表达，特别注意关于「nonverbals」的描述
  3. 跟读练习：大声朗读 3 遍，模拟 Amy Cuddy 在 TED 舞台上的演讲节奏 — 注意她使用「问题」（So how many of you…）、「停顿」和「重复」的技巧
  4. 脱稿复述：合上文本，用自己的话复述（中英文都可以）：
    • 什么是非语言沟通？
    • 肢体语言如何影响我们自己和他人？
  5. 结构化输出：用以下框架组织你的复述：

    • 我什么时候可以「power pose」？在什么场景下用得着？
    • 我的感受/看法？

🗣️ 口才小贴士

今日技巧：用「互动式提问」拉近与听众的距离

Amy Cuddy 在这段演讲的开头使用了一个非常有效的技巧：互动式提问。

她问：“So how many of you are sort of making yourselves smaller? Maybe you’re hunching, crossing your legs, maybe wrapping your ankles.”

她没有直接说教，而是先让听众「检查自己当下的姿势」，制造了参与感。这种技巧在职场演讲中特别有用：

  • 引导自查：先让听众/客户/同事反思自己的现状，再给出建议
  • 具体列举：给出具体的例子（hunching, crossing legs, wrapping ankles），而不是抽象地说「你可能姿势不好」
  • 拉近距离：用 “I see you”（我看到你了）来建立连接，让听众觉得被看见

应用练习：在你的下一次团队分享或汇报中，尝试用 1-2 个互动式提问开场：

  • “How many of you have felt overwhelmed by the amount of emails this week?”
  • “Quick show of hands — who here has struggled with …”

这样能立刻抓住注意力，而且让听众从被动接收变为主动参与。
