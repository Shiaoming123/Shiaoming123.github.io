📌 今日主题

商务演讲技巧 — 如何有力开场（How to Start a Presentation Strongly）

好的开场是一场成功演讲的一半。今天我们从 VirtualSpeech 的演讲指南中学习如何用英语自信地开启一场商务演讲，掌握开场的关键技巧和句式。

📝 英文原文

First impressions are everything — which means the start of your presentation acts as a tone-setter, drawing the audience in and interesting them in your message. Capturing people’s attention early makes it much more likely that they’ll pay attention throughout the presentation.

But how do you start a presentation in a way that ensures the audience is engaged? A lot depends on the type of presentation and the type of audience, but there are some common threads. Before anything else, wait until the majority of the audience are paying attention before introducing yourself and launching into your presentation. You don’t want to start by talking to a wall of inattentive people.

One effective technique is to open with a story. People are naturally drawn to narratives, so sharing a brief, relevant anecdote can instantly capture interest. Alternatively, you can start with a compelling statistic or a surprising fact. This can make the audience sit up and take notice. Another approach is to ask a rhetorical question that gets the audience thinking. Perhaps the most important thing is to make a strong connection between your opening and your core message. An engaging opening is only valuable if it leads naturally into what you actually want to talk about.

Your voice is a powerful tool. If you want to highlight something, raising your voice or speaking with intensity can help. Enunciate clearly, avoid mumbling, and don’t sound monotone. These are all ways to ensure your audience stays engaged with what you’re saying. Remember: the goal of your opening is to answer one key question for your audience — “Why should I listen to you?”

💡 实用表达

  1. “First impressions are everything.” — 第一印象至关重要。可用于任何商务场合，从面试到客户会议。
  2. “Let me start with a question…” — 用一个问题开场，立刻调动听众的思考。
  3. “Why should you care? Here’s why…” — 自问自答，直接点明”为什么这与你有关”。
  4. “I’d like to begin by sharing a quick story…” — 以简短故事开场，建立情感连接。
  5. “The goal of my talk today is to answer one key question…” — 明确演讲目标，告诉听众他们能带走什么。

🎯 练习步骤

  1. 通读全文：先完整读一遍英文原文，理解演讲开场的核心技巧
  2. 精读标记：逐句阅读，标记自己不熟悉的词汇和表达，重点关注”讲故事开头”、“引用数据”、“反问开场”三种技巧的英文表达
  3. 跟读练习：大声朗读 3 遍，特别注意断句的位置和语调的自然起伏
  4. 脱稿复述：合上文本，用中文或英文复述演讲开场的三个技巧
  5. 结构化输出：用以下框架组织你的复述：
    • 这段话在说什么？（一句话概括）
    • 主要观点是什么？（列 2-3 个开场技巧）
    • 我的感受/看法？（你平时演讲习惯怎么开场？有没有用得上的新技巧？）

🗣️ 口才小贴士

「黄金 30 秒规则」：听众在演讲前 30 秒就决定了要不要继续听。所以请记住这个公式：Hook（钩子）→ Relevance（相关性）→ Roadmap（路线图）。

具体做法：

  • Hook（0-5 秒）：一句震撼人心的话（数据、故事、反问）
  • Relevance（5-15 秒）：告诉听众”这与你有什么关系”
  • Roadmap（15-30 秒）：一句话说出今天的 3 个要点

下次如果你需要用英语做汇报，试试这样开场：“Let me start with a surprising fact. Did you know that 75% of people fear public speaking more than death? Today, I want to share three simple techniques to overcome that fear and deliver presentations with confidence.”
