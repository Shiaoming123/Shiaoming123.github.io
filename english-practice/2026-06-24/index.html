科技新闻：Slate Auto 发布低价电动皮卡，起售价仅 $24,950

📌 今日主题

科技新闻：Slate Auto 发布低价电动皮卡，起售价仅 $24,950 本文介绍了由 Jeff Bezos 投资的电动车初创公司 Slate Auto 推出的极具性价比的电动皮卡，讨论了其定价策略、产品特点以及市场定位。

📝 英文原文

Slate Auto, the EV startup backed by billionaire Jeff Bezos, has finally revealed the starting price of its electric truck: $24,950. Slate started taking preorders for the truck on Wednesday, June 24. Crucially, the company also said that it has boosted the estimated range of its base model from 150 miles to around 205 miles — although that comes at the cost of abandoning plans for a larger, 240-mile battery pack.

The aggressive pricing — half the average cost of a new car in the United States — puts Slate in position to capture a share of the lowest end of the new car market, which has few gas and even fewer electric options these days. The Chevrolet Bolt is one of Slate’s closest EV competitors on price, starting at around $29,000, while the Nissan Leaf starts at around $32,000. Ford has been teasing a $30,000 electric truck that is due in 2027.

The price reveal comes more than a year after Slate Auto emerged from stealth. Since then, the company has been steadily detailing the extremely basic, transforming EV, which starts as a two-seater pickup truck but can be modified into a five-seater SUV. The SUV version will start at $29,950.

Everything about the truck is deliberately minimal. It has hand-crank windows, lacks an infotainment system, and all orders start with the same gray composite material with no paint options. Instead, Slate plans to let buyers order customizable wraps for the vehicle, which helps cut out a major cost center — factory paint shops can run in the hundreds of millions of dollars. The company also said it “won’t have traditional dealerships” and plans to sell directly to customers, similar to Tesla and Rivian.

💡 实用表达

  1. “half the average cost” — 均价的一半。用于对比价格优势。例：“This phone costs half the average price of its competitors.”
  2. “put someone in position to…” — 使某人处于有利位置去做某事。例：“The new funding puts the company in position to expand globally.”
  3. “emerged from stealth” — 结束隐秘模式/浮出水面。科技行业常用语，形容公司从秘密研发到公开亮相。例：“The AI startup emerged from stealth with a groundbreaking product.”
  4. “deliberately minimal” — 刻意简约的。用于描述有意识地简化设计。例：“The app has a deliberately minimal interface to reduce distractions.”
  5. “at the cost of…” — 以…为代价。例：“They improved speed at the cost of battery life.”

🗣️ 口才小贴士

今日技巧：「用对比增强说服力」

今天文章大量使用了对比手法（“half the average cost”、“few gas and even fewer electric options”、“at the cost of…”），这让论点更有冲击力。在日常口语表达中，你可以主动使用对比来强化观点：

  • 先说一个常规情况：“Most electric cars cost over $35,000…”
  • 再用对比引出你的观点：“But Slate’s truck is half the average cost…”
  • 然后进一步对比对手：“Its closest competitor, the Chevrolet Bolt, still starts at $29,000.”

这种「常规 → 例外 → 对手对比」的三段式结构，能让你的表达更有层次感和说服力。下次讨论任何话题时，试试加入数据对比来支撑你的观点。
