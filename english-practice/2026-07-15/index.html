📝 英文原文（精华段落）

Sharon Brightwell heard her daughter crying down the line, and that was the end of any defence she might have mounted. The voice belonged to April, or so every instinct insisted: the same timbre, the same broken rhythm of a young woman in distress. The voice said she had been texting while driving, that she had hit a pregnant woman, that her phone had been seized by police. A man then took over the call, identifying himself as April’s attorney, and explained that bail would cost fifteen thousand dollars in cash. He warned Brightwell not to tell the bank what the money was for, because it might damage her daughter’s credit. Within the hour, the retiree from Dover, Florida had withdrawn the money and handed it to a courier she believed was connected to the courts. Only when she reached the real April, who had spent the morning at work and never been near a car accident, did she understand that her daughter had not made the call. No human had. The crying had been synthesised from a fragment of audio, and the daughter she thought she was rescuing existed only as a pattern of numbers in someone else’s machine.

Brightwell’s loss, reported across American local news in the summer of 2025, is now one of the most ordinary crimes in the United States. It is also one of the most technically advanced. The collision of those two facts — that a fraud requiring the absolute frontier of machine learning can be perpetrated against an ordinary grandmother in her kitchen, at scale, for the price of nothing — is the defining feature of a problem that law enforcement, banks, telecoms companies and regulators have spent two years failing to contain.

💡 实用表达

  1. “that was the end of any defence she might have mounted” — 她的防线瞬间崩溃了。形容一个人在情感冲击下完全失去防备的状态。
  2. “the collision of those two facts” — 这两个事实的碰撞。用于强调两个看似矛盾的情况同时存在。
  3. “at scale, for the price of nothing” — 大规模、零成本。非常有力的对比表达，强调技术让犯罪变得极其廉价。
  4. “the defining feature of a problem” — 一个问题的核心特征/定义性特点。用于分析问题时引出最关键的要素。
  5. “within the hour” — 一小时内。简洁有力，强调动作迅速。

🎯 练习步骤

  1. 通读全文：先完整读一遍英文原文，理解 Sharon Brightwell 被诈骗的故事
  2. 精读标记：逐句阅读，标记不熟悉的词汇和表达，特别注意情感描写的用词（crying, timbre, broken rhythm, distress）
  3. 跟读练习：大声朗读 3 遍，注意模仿叙述者的语气——故事讲述感（storytelling tone），不是干巴巴的新闻腔
  4. 脱稿复述：合上文本，用自己的话复述这个故事（中英文都可以）
  5. 结构化输出：用以下框架组织你的复述：
    • 这段话在说什么？（一句话概括）
    • 主要观点是什么？（列 2-3 个要点）
    • 我的感受/看法？（AI 技术进步带来的安全隐忧）

🗣️ 口才小贴士

讲故事开头法（The Anecdotal Opening）

今天的原文使用了一个非常经典的口才技巧：以一个具体人物的小故事开头，引出宏观议题。这种「个人故事 → 社会现象」的结构在 TED 演讲和新闻特写中极为常见。具体技巧拆解：

  1. 感官细节：第一句「heard her daughter crying down the line」立刻调动听觉——让听众仿佛也听到了那个声音。
  2. 具体数字：「fifteen thousand dollars」、「within the hour」——具体的数字让故事更加可信。
  3. 反转：「Only when she reached the real April… did she understand that her daughter had not made the call」——制造悬念然后揭开真相。
  4. 升华：最后一段从个人故事跳到宏观分析（Brightwell’s loss is now one of the most ordinary crimes），完成「个体 → 普遍」的过渡。

练习建议：试着用这种「一个小故事 → 大道理」的结构，讲述你身边的一个小观察，然后引申到一个更广泛的社会话题。比如：「我上周看到邻居老人被电话诈骗… 这让我想到，AI 时代我们该如何保护最脆弱的群体？」
