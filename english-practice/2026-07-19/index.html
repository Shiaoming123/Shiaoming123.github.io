📌 今日主题

名人演讲名段 — Charlie Chaplin《The Great Dictator》Final Speech（卓别林《大独裁者》结尾演讲）

这是电影史上最著名的演讲之一。1940年，查理·卓别林在影片《大独裁者》结尾发表了这段振聋发聩的演说。当时正值二战最黑暗的时期，卓别林饰演的犹太理发师被误认为独裁者 Hynkel，被迫登上讲台，却发表了呼吁自由、民主、人性与和平的演讲。这段台词由卓别林亲自撰写，反复修改，是英语修辞的巅峰之作。它的语言优美而有力，句式多样，适合高阶口语学习者深入研读和模仿。

📝 英文原文（全文）

Note: This is the complete text of Charlie Chaplin’s final speech from “The Great Dictator” (1940). The speech is delivered by a Jewish barber who has been mistaken for the dictatorial ruler Hynkel.

I’m sorry, but I don’t want to be an emperor. That’s not my business. I don’t want to rule or conquer anyone. I should like to help everyone — if possible — Jew, Gentile — black man — white. We all want to help one another. Human beings are like that. We want to live by each other’s happiness — not by each other’s misery. We don’t want to hate and despise one another. In this world there is room for everyone. And the good earth is rich and can provide for everyone. The way of life can be free and beautiful, but we have lost the way.

Greed has poisoned men’s souls, has barricaded the world with hate, has goose-stepped us into misery and bloodshed. We have developed speed, but we have shut ourselves in. Machinery that gives abundance has left us in want. Our knowledge has made us cynical. Our cleverness, hard and unkind. We think too much and feel too little. More than machinery we need humanity. More than cleverness we need kindness and gentleness. Without these qualities, life will be violent and all will be lost.

The aeroplane and the radio have brought us closer together. The very nature of these inventions cries out for the goodness in men — cries out for universal brotherhood — for the unity of us all. Even now my voice is reaching millions throughout the world — millions of despairing men, women, and little children — victims of a system that makes men torture and imprison innocent people.

To those who can hear me, I say — do not despair. The misery that is now upon us is but the passing of greed — the bitterness of men who fear the way of human progress. The hate of men will pass, and dictators die, and the power they took from the people will return to the people. And so long as men die, liberty will never perish.

Soldiers! Don’t give yourselves to brutes — men who despise you — enslave you — who regiment your lives — tell you what to do — what to think and what to feel! Who drill you — diet you — treat you like cattle, use you as cannon fodder. Don’t give yourselves to these unnatural men — machine men with machine minds and machine hearts! You are not machines! You are not cattle! You are men! You have the love of humanity in your hearts! You don’t hate! Only the unloved hate — the unloved and the unnatural! Soldiers! Don’t fight for slavery! Fight for liberty!

In the 17th Chapter of St Luke it is written: “The Kingdom of God is within man” — not one man nor a group of men, but in all men! In you! You, the people have the power — the power to create machines. The power to create happiness! You, the people, have the power to make this life free and beautiful, to make this life a wonderful adventure.

Then — in the name of democracy — let us use that power — let us all unite. Let us fight for a new world — a decent world that will give men a chance to work — that will give youth a future and old age a security. By the promise of these things, brutes have risen to power. But they lie! They do not fulfil that promise. They never will!

Dictators free themselves but they enslave the people! Now let us fight to fulfil that promise! Let us fight to free the world — to do away with national barriers — to do away with greed, with hate and intolerance. Let us fight for a world of reason, a world where science and progress will lead to all men’s happiness. Soldiers! In the name of democracy, let us all unite!

💡 实用表达

  1. “There is room for everyone.” — 每个人都有空间/一席之地。可用于职场、团队合作等场景。

    • 例：In a good team, there is room for different opinions.

  2. “We have lost the way.” — 我们迷失了方向。既可以指道德迷失，也可以指项目、人生方向。

    • 例：Our company has lost the way; we need to refocus on our core values.

  3. “We think too much and feel too little.” — 我们想得太多，感受得太少。可用于批判过度理性分析。

    • 例：In this data-driven world, sometimes we think too much and feel too little.

  4. “Do not despair.” — 不要绝望。简洁有力的鼓励语，比 “Don’t worry” 更有力量。

    • 例：The market is down, but do not despair — opportunities will come.

  5. “Do away with…” — 废除、摆脱。非常实用的动词短语。

    • 例：We need to do away with unnecessary bureaucracy.

🎯 练习步骤

  1. 通读全文：先完整读一遍英文原文，理解大意。注意这段演讲的”三段式”结构：开始的温和呼吁 → 中段的愤怒批判 → 结尾的激昂号召
  2. 精读标记：逐句阅读，标记不熟悉的词汇和表达。特别注意卓别林使用的排比（anaphora）和对比（antithesis）手法
  3. 跟读练习：先看一遍视频，模仿卓别林的语调、停顿和情感递进，然后大声朗读 3 遍
  4. 脱稿复述：合上文本，用自己的话复述大意（中英文都可以）
  5. 结构化输出：用以下框架组织你的复述：
    • 这段话在说什么？（一句话概括）
    • 演讲者的核心诉求是什么？（列 2-3 个要点）
    • 这个演讲为什么能打动人心？（分析修辞手法）
    • 我有什么感受或启发？

🗣️ 口才小贴士：排比的力量（Rule of Three）

卓别林在这篇演讲中大量使用了 排比（parallelism / anaphora） 和 “Rule of Three”（三点式排比）。这是英语演讲中最强大的修辞技巧之一。

经典例子：

“You are not machines! You are not cattle! You are men!”

这是一个完美的”三点式递进”：从否定到否定再到肯定的强烈翻转，每次重复都加强语气。

“Let us fight for a new world — a decent world that will give men a chance to work — that will give youth a future and old age a security.”

同样是三点式：从宏观（new world）到中观（decent world）到微观（具体承诺），层层递进。

🎯 练习方法：选择演讲中的一段排比句，先听原声感受节奏，然后自己练习时：

  1. 第一点：音量中等，语速正常
  2. 第二点：音量稍大，语速减慢
  3. 第三点（高潮）：音量最大，每个词之间有明显停顿，用坚定的语气收尾

日常应用：在职场汇报、即兴发言中，有意识使用”三点式”结构组织你的观点。比如：

“We need three things: first, a clear vision; second, the right team; and third, the discipline to execute.”
