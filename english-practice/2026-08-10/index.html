每日英语口语练习：2026-08-10（星期一）

周一 TED 演讲片段：Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie《The Danger of a Single Story》（单一故事的危险）——TEDGlobal 2009，播放量超 4,250 万次，TED 史上最受欢迎的演讲之一。尼日利亚小说家阿迪契以亲身经历讲述「单一故事」如何制造刻板印象、剥夺人的尊严，观点犀利、叙事动人，是「个人叙事 + 论点推进」型演讲的教科书。与周日 Steve Jobs 的「美音叙事」不同，这是奈及利亚口音英语（Nigerian English）的绝佳听力素材。

1. 📖 原文（核心段落，约 240 词）

演讲者：Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie（奇玛曼达·恩戈兹·阿迪契，尼日利亚小说家，著有《紫木槿》《半个黄太阳》《美国佬》） 场合：TEDGlobal 2009，英国牛津，2009 年 7 月 选取段落：演讲第 3 部分「权力与单一故事」（Power and the Single Story）+ 结尾论点段。文本依据 TED 官方 transcript 整理。

So that is how to create a single story: show a people as one thing, as only one thing, over and over again, and that is what they become.

It is impossible to talk about the single story without talking about power. There is a word, an Igbo word, that I think about whenever I think about the power structures of the world, and it is “nkali.” It’s a noun that loosely translates to “to be greater than another.” Like our economic and political worlds, stories too are defined by the principle of nkali: how they are told, who tells them, when they’re told, how many stories are told, are really dependent on power. Power is the ability not just to tell the story of another person, but to make it the definitive story of that person.

The single story creates stereotypes, and the problem with stereotypes is not that they are untrue, but that they are incomplete. They make one story become the only story. Of course, Africa is a continent full of catastrophes: there are immense ones, such as the horrific rapes in Congo, and depressing ones, such as the fact that 5,000 people apply for one job vacancy in Nigeria. But there are other stories that are not about catastrophe, and it is very important, it is just as important, to talk about them. I’ve always felt that it is impossible to engage properly with a place or a person without engaging with all of the stories of that place and that person. The consequence of the single story is this: it robs people of dignity. It makes our recognition of our equal humanity difficult. It emphasizes how we are different rather than how we are similar.

4. 💬 跟读练习建议

  1. 先听三遍：第一遍无字幕，感受她讲故事时的语速起伏（讲 Fide 家故事时慢，讲论点时加快）；第二遍开英文字幕核对；第三遍闭眼只听「nkali」段。
  2. 意群切分（Chunking）：长句切成 3–5 词块，如 Power is the ability / not just to tell the story / of another person, / but to make it / the definitive story / of that person，先慢后快。
  3. 影子跟读（Shadowing）：选「The single story creates stereotypes…」段，落后原声 0.5 秒跟读；阿迪契发音清晰、重音分明，是练习「重音节奏」（stress-timed rhythm）的好素材。
  4. 排比句练习（Tricolon）：It robs people of dignity. / It makes our recognition of our equal humanity difficult. / It emphasizes how we are different rather than how we are similar. —— 三个 It 开头，层层递进，练「两抑一扬」。
  5. 金句打磨：The problem with stereotypes is not that they are untrue, but that they are incomplete. —— 这是全篇最容易被引用的一句，练到脱口而出。

5. 🔄 即兴口语练习

Prompt 1（90 秒）：Your single story —— 讲一个你曾对某地/某群体「只有单一印象」、后来被打破的经历，用上 single story / stereotype / I had bought into。

Prompt 2（60 秒）：用三个 It 开头的排比句，写一段你自己的观点结尾（模仿原文句式）。例：It makes us lazy listeners. It closes the door to surprise. It keeps us small. —— 然后大声说三遍。

Prompt 3（90 秒）：观点题——“Do social media algorithms give us a single story of the world?” 给出立场 + 两个理由（可用 On one hand… / But… 展开）。

Prompt 4（60 秒）：用英文向朋友解释什么是 nkali，并举一个现实中的例子。

6. 📋 发音技巧提示

1. Nigerian English 口音特征（本周听力重点）

  • 阿迪契说的是受过教育的尼日利亚英语：元音偏「单元音化」（diphthong 收窄，如 story 的 /ɔː/ 更紧），节奏比美音更「音节等时」（syllable-timed）。不必模仿口音，但要听懂：重点抓重音词即可。

2. 重音即意义（Stress = Meaning）

  • 对比句 not… but… 结构，重音落在转折词后：not that they are unTRUE, / but that they are incomPLETE。重读「新信息」，弱读「旧信息」。

3. 三连排比节奏（Tricolon Rhythm）

  • It robs people of dignity. / It makes… difficult. / It emphasizes… —— 前两句升调（未完成感），第三句降调收束（完成感）。这是英语演讲最经典的节奏型。

4. 停顿即强调（The Pause）

  • show a people as one thing, / as ONLY one thing, / over and over again, / and that is what they become. —— 每个逗号处稍作停顿，only 重读。停顿让听众「接住」你的话。

5. 连读与弱读（Linking & Weak Forms）

  • talk‿about, kind‿of, apply‿for, one‿of 连读；of, and, that, are 弱读。注意 apply for one job vacancy 中 for 读 /fər/。

6. 尾音辅音收干净

  • power, story, dignity, incomplete 词尾辅音要收住，不要加多余的 /ə/（避免「啵儿」音）。Nigerian English 常见加音问题，练时留意。

“The problem with stereotypes is not that they are untrue, but that they are incomplete.” — 好的口才不是把话说满，而是敢于说：事情还有另一面。
